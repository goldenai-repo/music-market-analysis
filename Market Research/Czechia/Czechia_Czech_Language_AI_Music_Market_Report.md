### 2.3 Demand Conclusion

Demand Rating: **Medium-High**

Czechia has **10.7 million** people and **10.1 million** internet users, with **94.2%** internet penetration. YouTube ads reach **7.99 million** people, equal to **79.4%** of the local internet-user base, which is strong enough for Czech-language music testing. TikTok reach is smaller at **2.01 million** adults, so TikTok should be treated as a secondary viral-discovery channel rather than the primary market-size indicator.
