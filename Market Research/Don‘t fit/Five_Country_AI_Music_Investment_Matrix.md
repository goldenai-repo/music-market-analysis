## 3. Scoring Rules

Scores range from 1-5, with higher scores indicating greater suitability for priority testing. Because this matrix is evaluating AI music localization rather than a traditional music-market launch, AI interest and platform fit are weighted more heavily than in a standard music-market model.

| Dimensions | Weight | What a High Score Means |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| AI Interest Index | 25% | Local search interest in AI music tools is high and sustained |
| Market demand | 20% | Sufficient population, internet users, target-language audience, and YouTube reach for testing |
| Content supply opportunities | 20% | Local-language supply appears limited, creating a content gap |
| Monetization ability | 15% | Market has streaming revenue, paid music consumption, or spending-power signals |
| Platform fit | 10% | Strong local access to Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music, Anghami, or other testable platforms |
| Low competition opportunities | 10% | Visible local creator competition is not saturated |

Comprehensive score calculation:

`AI Interest x 25% + Demand x 20% + Supply Opportunity x 20% + Monetization x 15% + Platform Fit x 10% + Low Competition x 10%`
