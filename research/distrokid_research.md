# DistroKid Research

## Overview

DistroKid is a music distribution platform that helps artists release finished songs to streaming platforms and online stores. Unlike Suno, which focuses on creating music, DistroKid focuses on distributing music after it has already been made.

In this project, DistroKid represents the distribution side of the AI music workflow. A creator could generate a song with an AI music tool like Suno, then use DistroKid to publish the finished track to platforms such as Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, Amazon Music, TikTok, and Instagram.

---

## How Artists Publish Music Through DistroKid

The basic publishing workflow:

1. The artist creates a DistroKid account and chooses a paid plan.
2. The artist uploads the audio file and cover artwork.
3. The artist enters release information, such as song title, artist name, genre, release date, and songwriter details.
4. The artist selects the platforms and stores for distribution.
5. DistroKid delivers the release to the selected platforms.
6. After the song goes live, the artist can manage the release and track earnings through DistroKid.

During sign-up, DistroKid asks users to choose a paid plan before entering the full upload workflow. This shows that DistroKid is mainly a paid subscription-based distribution service.

---

## Pricing and Features

DistroKid uses an annual subscription model. The main plans shown on the pricing page are Musician, Musician Plus, and Ultimate.

| Plan | Price | Main Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Musician | $24.99 billed annually | Basic release essentials |
| Musician Plus | $44.99 billed annually | More release customization |
| Ultimate | $89.99 billed annually | More data and management features |

### Key Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Music distribution | Sends songs to major streaming platforms and stores. |
| Unlimited uploads | Paid plans allow unlimited song uploads. |
| Release metadata | Artists enter song title, artist name, genre, release date, and songwriter details. |
| Release customization | Higher-tier plans support custom release dates, label names, and pricing options. |
| Streaming stats | Some plans include daily streaming stats and analytics. |
| Royalty splits | Artists can split earnings with collaborators. |
| Promotion tools | Tools like Promo Cards, Spotify Canvas Generator, and HyperFollow help promote releases. |

---

## Royalty Handling

DistroKid helps collect earnings from streaming platforms and music stores. Artists can receive their earnings after the platforms take their share.

DistroKid also supports royalty splits, which allows artists to divide earnings with collaborators such as producers, vocalists, songwriters, or other contributors.

---

## Initial Observations

### Strengths

- Makes music distribution easier for independent artists
- Supports many major streaming platforms
- Uses a clear annual subscription model
- Allows unlimited uploads under paid plans
- Provides royalty collection and split tools
- Includes promotion tools after release

### Limitations

- Users need a paid plan before accessing the full upload workflow
- Artists still need finished audio and cover artwork
- Some customization analytics features require higher-tier plans
- AI-generated music may need extra attention to copyright and ownership

---

## Relevance to AI-Generated Music

DistroKid is relevant because AI-generated songs still need a way to reach listeners. Suno can help create a song, and DistroKid can help distribute the finished track to streaming platforms.

Together, they show two parts of the AI music ecosystem: music creation and music distribution.
