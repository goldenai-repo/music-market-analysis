# Turkish YouTube Review Guidelines

| Field | Guideline |
|---|---|
| manual_valid | yes = valid song-level Turkish music sample for downstream analysis; no = tutorial, DJ mix, compilation, or other non-song content. |
| language_verified | yes = Turkish language or clearly Turkish music context; no = not Turkish; unsure = cannot confidently verify. |
| is_music_video | yes = song, official audio, music video, or music performance; no = tutorial, commentary, news, or demonstration. |
| ai_suno_related | yes = explicit AI-generated, AI-assisted, AI-cover, or Suno-related indication; no = no explicit AI evidence; unsure = ambiguous evidence. |
| channel_type | Categorize the source channel as AI music channel, AI cover channel, official artist channel, traditional/archive channel, DJ/mix channel, or music reinterpretation channel. |
