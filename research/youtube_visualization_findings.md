# YouTube Visualization Findings

## Sample

The current YouTube sample is based on the query `lofi study music` and includes 10 videos.

## Metrics
- `view_count` is used as an exposure indicator.
- `like_count` is used as an audience response indicator.
- `comment_count` is used as an additional engagement indicator.
- `engagement_rate` is calculated as `(like_count + comment_count) / view_count`.

## Initial Findings
- View count and like count are generally aligned in the current 10-video sample. The video with the highest view count also has the highest like count.
- One video dominates both views and likes, suggesting that raw engagement volume is strongly influenced by exposure.
- Engagement rate shows a different ranking from raw views and likes because it normalizes likes and comments by view count.
- A higher engagement rate indicates stronger audience response relative to view volume.
- YouTube engagement metrics can complement the iTunes metadata baseline by adding public performance signals such as views, likes, comments, and engagement rate.
