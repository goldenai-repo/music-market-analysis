# Suno Research

## Overview

Suno is an AI music generation platform that allows users to create complete songs from text prompts. Users can describe the theme, mood, genre, or style of a song, and Suno generates music based on the input.

The platform is designed to lower the barrier to music creation by allowing users to generate music without traditional music production skills. It can be useful for casual creators, content creators, independent artists, and users who want quick music demos or background tracks.

This section summarizes Suno's product features, workflow, user experience, limitations, and a small music generation experiment.

---

## Product Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Prompt-based generation | Users describe the desired song through a text prompt. |
| Simple and Advanced modes | Users can choose a simple creation flow or use Advanced mode for more control. |
| Full song generation | Suno can generate vocals, lyrics, instrumentation, and production. |
| Custom lyrics / instrumental option | Users can add lyrics or generate instrumental-only music. |
| Audio, Voice, and Inspo inputs | Users can provide audio, voice, or inspiration references to guide generation. |
| Workspace and library | Generated songs are saved for review, playback, and management. |
| Credit-based system | Free plans provide limited credits, while paid plans unlock more credits and advanced models/features. |

---

## How Suno Works

1. The user enters a song description prompt.
2. The user selects either Simple mode or Advanced mode.
3. The user can optionally add lyrics, audio references, voice references, or inspiration references.
4. Suno generates one or more song variations based on the prompt and settings.
5. Generated songs are saved in the user's workspace/library for review, playback, and refinement.

---

## Initial Observations

### Strengths

- Fast music generation process
- Low barrier to music creation
- Multiple input methods, including prompt, lyrics, audio, voice, and inspo
- Easy experimentation with different prompts and styles
- Generated songs are automatically saved in the workspace/library

### Limitations

- Free plans provide limited credits
- Some advanced models or full-song access require paid upgrades
- Output quality may vary between generations
- Users may need to regenerate songs multiple times to achieve the desired result
