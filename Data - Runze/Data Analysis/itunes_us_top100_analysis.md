# iTunes US Top 100 Data Analysis


## Methodology

- Scope: Rank 1-100, totaling 100 song records.
- Artist: artist credits such as `Artist A, Artist B & Artist C` are split into individual artists and counted separately.
- Genre: genres are split by comma, and the generic Apple category `Music` is excluded.
- Year: only the year is extracted from `Release Date`; blank dates are counted as `Missing date`.
