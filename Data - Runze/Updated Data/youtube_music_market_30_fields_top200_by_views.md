# YouTube Music Market Top 200 by Views

Sorted by `view_count`. Data source: YouTube Data API and calculated/inferred fields.

## Methodology Note

This dataset is designed to reduce the issue that a YouTube video is not always the same thing as a music work. We handle this in four ways:

1. **Layered candidate collection**: Instead of relying on one broad search query, we collect candidates across general music-video terms, genre-specific terms, and channel or label terms. This improves coverage of official music videos, official audio, lyric videos, visualizers, and music content from different genres and regions.

2. **Video type classification**: YouTube music content can appear in several formats, such as official music videos, official audio, lyric videos, visualizers, live performances, covers, and fan uploads. The `video_type` field separates these formats so they are not interpreted as the same kind of music-market unit.

3. **Official-source confidence scoring**: We use signals such as the video title, channel name, YouTube music category, and channel metrics to estimate whether a video is likely to be official music content. The `official_confidence_score` helps reduce the influence of fan uploads, covers, reuploads, reactions, and other less reliable samples.

4. **Future song-level grouping**: A single music work may have multiple YouTube versions, such as an official music video, official audio, lyric video, and live version. At this stage, the dataset remains video-level, but the classification and confidence fields make it possible to later group multiple official videos into song-level records using artist and song-title matching.
